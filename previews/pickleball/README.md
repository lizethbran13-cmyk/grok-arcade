# Grok Pickleball Park

A giant walk-in sports park built around real pickleball, made for David.

- **Real rules:** diagonal underhand serve, the two-bounce rule, the kitchen (no volleys in the non-volley zone), and side-out scoring to 11, win by 2. Play singles, or doubles with an AI partner (Coach Kai, Luna, Snowie or Rally Rita).
- **Shots:** dink, third-shot drop, drive, lob and smash. Spin depends on your paddle (the MaxGrit Signature has the most).
- **Touch controls:** tap to hit. Swipe up for soft shots and drives, way up for a lob, down for a smash, and sideways to aim. Optional auto-move and auto-swing make it kid-friendly.
- **Places:**
  - Stadium Court, with Umpire Pi-rat
  - 8 open courts
  - Night Lights Court
  - Quiet Practice Court, with Silent Serve foam balls and Candy on patrol
  - Torty's Pro Shop (Martina, a.k.a. Torty)
  - The Clubhouse: Park Ladder, tournaments, trophy hall and locker room
  - Dill-icious Snack Shack (Snowie)
  - Training Center, with drills run by Coach Luna
- **Career:** coins, a rating from 2.0 to 5.5, a 10-rung Park Ladder up to the Pickle King, and 4 tournaments.
- **Engine:** the Turbo engine (three.js) with PBR, soft shadows, GTAO and bloom, plus first- and third-person views.
