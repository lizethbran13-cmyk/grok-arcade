# Grok Snowie Sky Tours (beta)

Fly Snowie's tour copter over a big open valley:

- 🐶 **Pup Town**: the Big Bone Arch, the Giant Hydrant water tower and the Doghouse Town Hall.
- 🐢 **Torty Town**: the Great Torty Statue, the Shell Dome Museum and the Lakeside Lighthouse.
- 🎄 **North Pole Village**: the Candy Cane Pole, the Toy Workshop and Snowcap Peak.
- Pi-rat's pirate ship on the lake.

Things to do:

- 📸 Visit 10 tour stops and collect a postcard at each one.
- 🐾 Rescue 6 stranded pets and fly them home.
- 🏁 Race 3 ring courses for gold medals.
- 🛂 Collect 5 passport stamps.
- 💥 Crash landings are harmless: a tow-drone carries you to the nearest pad, and your first crash earns the Survival Badge.
- 🛬 Land on a pad, hop out and walk into every building in town.
- All 5 pets appear: Snowie, Candy, Torty, Luna and Pi-rat.

Built on the Turbo engine: PBR, soft shadows, GTAO and bloom. Far-only detail culling and quantized geometry keep it within iPhone memory. There are touch controls for iPhone Safari in portrait.

**Status:** beta / building now. This game is not shipped yet.
