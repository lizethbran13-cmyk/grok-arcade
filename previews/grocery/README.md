# Grok Grocery Tycoon (beta)

Run a store in the town of Grokton. You can work your way up from bagging groceries, or start at the top and build a whole retail empire.

- **Two ways to play**
  - **Career:** start as a courtesy clerk. Bag groceries, push carts, stock shelves, clean spills and help shoppers. Promotions take you from Clerk to Cashier, Department Lead, Assistant Manager, Store Manager and finally **Owner**. You can clerk at any type of store.
  - **Boss:** start in charge of your first store, then buy lots, hire staff, set prices, order stock and grow a chain.
- **11 store types**, each with its own walk-in interior, products, layout and staff tasks:
  - Supermarket
  - Supercenter (groceries plus general merchandise)
  - Neighborhood Market
  - Convenience Store
  - Pharmacy
  - Shoe Store
  - Toy Store
  - Electronics
  - Pet Store
  - Clothing
  - Home Goods

  You can remodel a store into another type.
- **Empire:** mix store types across the town map. Also:
  - a distribution center with delivery trucks
  - the Grok Goods store brand
  - daily hiring
  - a bank loan
  - goals and news
- **Events:** holiday rushes, price wars, health inspections, freezer failures, register jams and VIP pet shoppers.
- **Enterable rival stores:** walk into any of them to browse their aisles, check prices, read their deals flyer and match prices. The rivals are:
  - Bargain Bonanza
  - QuickCart Express
  - MegaMart Supercenter
  - Shopping Row, a strip of six specialty shops: ToyTopia, Sole Patrol, ZapZone, PawPals, Fit & Fab and PillBox
- **First-person mode:** tap 👁️ VIEW, or press V on a keyboard. On iPhone, drag to look and use the stick to walk. You can work the registers and stock shelves from eye level.
- **16 mini-games:**
  - Bag It Right
  - Beep! Scanner
  - Stock Sprint
  - Mop Mania
  - Produce Pyramid
  - Deli Slicer
  - Cupcake Frosting
  - Truck Tetris
  - Perfect Fit
  - Gift Wrap Rush
  - Tech Setup
  - Pharmacy Sort
  - Pet Feed
  - Fold It
  - Home Staging
  - Coffee Bar
- **Partners and cameos:** Ratita Industries, Tortuga Tech and Ruffsources are your suppliers. Luna, Pi-rat, Snowie, Candy and Torty all show up, and the regulars sometimes call Martina "Torty".

It's built on the Turbo engine. Geometry is quantized and freed from the CPU once it's on the GPU, and fixtures are instanced, so the game fits comfortably in iPhone Safari memory. Touch controls are designed for portrait.

**Status:** beta / building now. Not shipped.
