# Grok Ruffsources HQ (beta)

Your first day at **Ruffsources**, the HR department that Ratita Industries and Tortuga Tech Inc. can't live without. It's run by Candy, a miniature schnauzer who naps on the paperwork.

- Walk into every room of the HQ: Lobby, Mail Room, Break Room, Filing Archive, Interview Room and Candy's office. You can also visit the Ratita Industries branch, the Tortuga Tech test lab and the Bark & Brew café.
- Jobs:
  - Mail sorting
  - Snack orders
  - File It!
  - Gadget Returns
  - Drop Tower
  - Pupuccino Pour
  - Interviewing new hires
- Solve the mystery of Candy's missing squeaky toy.
- Spot Candy napping on her doormat in 6 places, and find 26 Golden Bones.
- Cameos: Candy, Luna, Pi-rat, Snowie and Torty.

Built on the Turbo engine: PBR, soft shadows, GTAO and bloom. Geometry is quantized so it fits in iPhone memory, and there are touch controls for iPhone Safari in portrait.

**Status:** beta / building now. This game is not shipped yet.
